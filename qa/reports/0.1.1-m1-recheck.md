**QA-20 · S4 · Gate Lighthouse step is single-run**
- **What happened:** in the gate run, `/about` perf scored 74 (FCP 4.4 s on a loaded host, right after the parallel e2e steps). Three isolated re-runs scored 84–88. The build is identical and TBT and CLS are 0, so this is measurement variance, not a regression.
- **Owner:** QA (`qa/scripts/lighthouse.sh`).
- **Proposed fix:** take the median of 3 runs per URL (Lighthouse's documented variance practice), or run Lighthouse before the parallel e2e steps. This needs the Director's OK on the method, so the gate was not changed in this re-check.

**Observations (not bugs, for Creative):**
- The "Bigger map" toggle uses a magnifier icon.
- `/about` best-practices is 96 because of `image-size-responsive` (the OneMap logo is low-res for DPR).
