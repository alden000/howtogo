- Real Android Chrome users (the main audience) see this jump each time they open Settings, until they dismiss the card.

  - the APK served by a local Worker: smoke:deploy 38/38, including the AC 39 SHA check;

- The other gate failures are host-load flakes or a flaky dev test (QA-76).
