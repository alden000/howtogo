**QA-7 · S3 · Focusable elements inside the aria-hidden map (axe `aria-hidden-focus`, serious)**
- Repro: Route detail at ≥ 720 px wide.

- Fix: `tabindex=-1` on the canvas / `inert`; keep the attribution outside the hidden node.
- Test: `mx: *-720`, `*-1440`.
