## Must fix

Use the existing field-error pattern from `family.tsx` instead: `<div class={`field2${err ? ' bad' : ''}`}>` (3 px ink border), with the message inside the field as `<span class="fnote" id="{id}-err" role="alert"><Icon name="info" />…</span>`. Keep `aria-invalid` and `aria-describedby`. Remove the inline colour.

**F2. Move focus when the picker opens.** "不对？换一个" on the confirm card and the H1 nudge both swap the card for the picker, and nothing tells a screen-reader user. Give the picker title `<b class="t-headline" tabIndex={-1}>` and focus it on mount (and again when the confirm card comes back after a pick). No extra announcement is needed, because the title is the announcement.

## Should fix (small)

- **S1. `lang="en"`** on the row text `<span class="tx" lang="en">` in the block list. The street and "Singapore 123456" are English, so a zh screen reader otherwise reads them with the Chinese voice. The confirm card already does this.
- **S2. Picker icon in My places.** The header always shows `home`. For `place-block`, use `loc` on `c-surface-2` (the P8 "else" tint). Keep `home` and `tint-home` for `home-block`.
- **S3. Nudge semantics.** `<p class="note" role="status">` is a standing offer, not a status change. Use `<div class="note">` with no role, so it isn't read out as an update every time Home loads.
- **S4. Nudge at 200 %.** Add `flex-wrap: wrap` to the nudge row (or a `.note.nudge` rule) so 不用了 / No thanks drops under the text instead of squeezing it. QA: check 200 % zh/en at 390 px for no horizontal scroll and both targets ≥ 56 px.
- **S5. H10 back-fill.** Approved: it fills quietly, with no spinner and no announcement, and the card stays light. Not repeating a street line that matches the name is correct. Nothing changes offline.
